Is Anyone There?

A master's thesis project investigating how much of Bluesky's activity comes from automated accounts — and whether the claims of Dead Internet Theory hold up to measurement.

Why this topic

The question of how much online activity is genuinely human has become harder to ignore as AI-generated content grows. I wanted to approach it empirically rather than speculatively — to build a tool that makes actual measurements instead of relying on intuition or anecdote. Dead Internet Theory is easy to dismiss as a conspiracy theory, but the underlying concern — that bots distort what we see and how we interact online — is worth taking seriously.
