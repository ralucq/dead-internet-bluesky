Enter a Bluesky handle. The page compares the account with declared bots and ordinary accounts from a study on the Dead Internet Theory.

How this works, and what it cannot tell you

The page reads the account's public profile, its latest 100 posts, replies and reposts, and up to 100 followers and 100 followed accounts. It turns them into 37 measurements (timing, repetition, replies, followers and so on) and compares them with 598 accounts that call themselves bots and 580 ordinary accounts. Everything is computed in your browser. Nothing is stored and nothing is sent anywhere except to Bluesky's public API.

The result is a level of evidence, not a verdict. It measures how much the account looks like the declared bots of the study, which are mostly scheduled feed accounts (news, weather, alerts). It cannot see AI-driven or fake-person accounts that behave like people and do not say what they are. An ordinary account can also look like a bot (for example one that posts only links on a schedule). Never use the result to accuse a person.
